# Asset Research: Tile Packs & Character Sprites

Research conducted 2026-04-10 for the FF1-Style NES RPG project.

---

## Requirements

- **Overworld tiles**: 16x16 pixel art for world map (grass, water, mountains, forests, towns, etc.)
- **Character sprites**: SNES-style with multiple animation frames (walk, attack, idle), usable on the overworld
- **Aesthetic**: NES/SNES Final Fantasy style
- **License**: Must support commercial or open-source use

---

## Overworld Tile Packs

### Free Options

#### 1. Open RPG Fantasy Tilesets — finalbossblues
- **URL:** https://finalbossblues.itch.io/openrtp-tiles
- **Tile size:** 16x16
- **License:** CC0 (public domain)
- **Price:** Free
- **Included:** Complete game world — overworld, town/castle exteriors, interiors, dungeons, ship. RPG Maker 2K/3 chipset format.
- **Notes:** Best overall free value. CC0 means zero licensing concerns. Covers every environment type an FF1 clone needs. Uses indexed color with pink background (needs transparency conversion). Rated 4.9/5.

#### 2. Worldmap/Overworld Tileset — MrBeast (OpenGameArt)
- **URL:** https://opengameart.org/content/worldmapoverworld-tileset
- **Tile size:** 16x16 (water animation is 32x32)
- **License:** CC-BY 3.0
- **Price:** Free
- **Included:** Grass, forests, mountains (3x3 grid), water (animated, 15 frames), sand, snow, paths, coastline transitions.
- **Notes:** Closest match to FF1's overworld aesthetic. 240 favorites on OpenGameArt. Missing structures (castles/towns) — pair with another set for buildings.

#### 3. 16x16 Overworld Tiles — Sharm (OpenGameArt)
- **URL:** https://opengameart.org/content/16x16-overworld-tiles
- **Tile size:** 16x16
- **License:** CC-BY 3.0 / CC-BY 4.0
- **Price:** Free
- **Included:** Grass, water, sand, snow, forests, paths.
- **Notes:** Clean, simple pixel art. Lacks buildings/structures and terrain variety. Good as a supplement.

#### 4. Roguelike/RPG Pack (1,700+ tiles) — Kenney (OpenGameArt)
- **URL:** https://opengameart.org/content/roguelikerpg-pack-1700-tiles
- **Tile size:** 16x16
- **License:** CC0
- **Price:** Free
- **Included:** Floors, walls, roofs, trees, doors, furniture, mining supplies, flags, UI elements.
- **Notes:** Massive tile count but style is more roguelike/cute than NES-JRPG. Better for interiors/dungeons than overworld.

### Paid Options

#### 5. NES Style TileSet — Marumugi4510
- **URL:** https://koji-marumugi.itch.io/nes-style-tile-set
- **Tile size:** 16x16 (also 48x48 for RPG Maker MV/MZ)
- **License:** Commercial and personal use OK. No redistribution.
- **Price:** $7
- **Included:** Field/overworld, castle & town exterior, snow variant, building interior, dungeon interior, objects, combat backgrounds.
- **Notes:** Uses actual NES color palette with NES color restrictions. Most authentic NES-style option. Combat backgrounds are a perfect fit for the existing battle system. Rated 5.0/5.

#### 6. Lo-Bit RPG Pixel Assets — finalbossblues
- **URL:** https://finalbossblues.itch.io/lo-bit-pack
- **Tile size:** 8x8 terrain / 16x16 objects
- **License:** Commercial use OK
- **Price:** $20
- **Included:** Complete environment tiles, 20 character sprites with walk animations, 64+ additional characters, animated objects, bonus overworld tiles, bonus menu window-skin. 170+ characters total.
- **Notes:** All-in-one pack. Explicitly tagged "8-Bit" and "NES". Rated 5.0/5. The 8x8 terrain base creates authentic NES texture.

#### 7. World Map Tileset — AlexPixels
- **URL:** https://alexdraws.itch.io/world-map-tileset
- **Tile size:** 16x16 (includes 2x and 3x scaled versions)
- **License:** Extended license (commercial OK)
- **Price:** $14.99
- **Included:** Water, beaches, grass, snow, wasteland, desert, 6 forest types, 6 mountain types, volcano, cave entrances, castles, palaces, lairs, houses, temples, cities, towers, pyramids, bridges, crystals, clouds. Animated water.
- **Notes:** Most comprehensive world map tileset found. More SNES/GBA than strict NES. Users describe it as giving "Final Fantasy vibes." Rated 4.7/5.

#### 8. World Map Tiles [16x16] — MalibuDarby/VexedEnigma
- **URL:** https://vexedenigma.itch.io/world-map-tiles
- **Tile size:** 16x16
- **License:** Royalty-free, commercial OK
- **Price:** $2.99
- **Included:** Three biomes (grassland, desert, snow), trees, mountains, swamps, villages, pyramids, towers.
- **Notes:** GBA-inspired (Mother 3, Zelda Minish Cap). Affordable. Rated 4.6/5.

## Character Sprite Packs

### Free Options

#### 1. 16x16 RPG Character Sprite Sheet — javikolog
- **URL:** https://route1rodent.itch.io/16x16-rpg-character-sprite-sheet
- **Sprite size:** 16x16
- **Animations:** 3-frame walk cycle, 4 directions
- **Characters:** ~12-15 in two styles (NES-style and redrawn "new style")
- **License:** CC BY-SA 4.0 (commercial OK, share-alike, attribution required)
- **Price:** Free
- **Notes:** Perfect 16x16 size. Includes layered Aseprite source files for customization. Share-alike requirement may be restrictive. Limited character count.

#### 2. RPG Character Sprites — GrafxKid (OpenGameArt)
- **URL:** https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-character-sprites
- **Sprite size:** ~16x16
- **Animations:** 3-frame walk cycle
- **Characters:** Small set + template for derivation
- **License:** CC0 (public domain)
- **Price:** Free
- **Notes:** Most permissive license. Template character enables creating custom classes. Very small base set — best combined with other packs.

#### 3. PIPOYA FREE RPG Character Sprites — Pipoya
- **URL:** https://pipoya.itch.io/pipoya-free-rpg-character-sprites-32x32
- **Sprite size:** 32x32
- **Animations:** 4-directional walking only
- **Characters:** 64 characters, 4 skin color variants each. Includes CharaMEL generator.
- **License:** Commercial and personal use OK
- **Price:** Free
- **Notes:** 32x32 is double the game's tile size — would need scaling or accommodation. No attack animations. Best as prototyping/placeholder.

### Paid Options

#### 4. Time Fantasy RPG Sprites 1 (80+ Characters) — finalbossblues
- **URL:** https://finalbossblues.itch.io/tf-rpg-charactersprites-1
- **Sprite size:** 16x32 (16x16 tile base, two tiles tall)
- **Animations:** 4-directional walk (2 frames + neutral), emotion animations (nod, shake head, laugh, surprise), 3-frame weapon/action poses
- **Characters:** 80+ total — 32 heroes/villains, 24 military, 16 NPCs (recolored to 32), animals, treasure chests
- **License:** Commercial use OK. No redistribution.
- **Price:** $15
- **Notes:** Best overall match. Explicitly designed to evoke "classic RPGs of the SNES era." 16x16 tile base matches the game exactly. Includes warriors, mages, knights, military factions. Separate Side-View Battlers expansion ($7.99) adds combat animations. Mini Sprites pack ($1-2) adds 120+ 16x16 world map sprites.

#### 5. Time Elements: Character Core Set — finalbossblues
- **URL:** https://finalbossblues.itch.io/time-elements-character-core-set
- **Sprite size:** 16x32 (16x16 tile base)
- **Animations:** 4-directional walk, crouch, jump, attack (with weapons)
- **Characters:** 64+ modular pieces, 5 premade heroes, 16 premade NPCs. Includes character generator tool.
- **License:** Commercial use OK. No redistribution.
- **Price:** $20
- **Notes:** Modular system is ideal for generating 12 distinct character classes. Swap hairstyles, armor, weapons. Has built-in attack animations. Tagged "SNES" and "JRPG". Two expansion packs available.

#### 6. 16x16 Puny Characters Editor Edition — Shade
- **URL:** https://merchant-shade.itch.io/16x16-puny-characters-plus-sprites
- **Sprite size:** 16x16 (32x32 canvas)
- **Animations:** 8-directional idle, walk, carry, jump, sword attack, bow attack, stave attack, throw, hurt, death
- **Characters:** Modular paperdoll system, 13+ races, multiple skin tones, extensive equipment options
- **License:** Commercial use OK, no credit required
- **Price:** $10 (often 50% off)
- **Notes:** Best animation variety — sword, bow, and stave attacks map to Warrior/Thief/Mage archetypes. Includes GIMP/Photoshop layer files. Matching tileset packs available (Puny World, Puny Dungeon). Slightly more chibi than pure SNES FF.

#### 7. Pixel Art RPG Character Creator — edermunizz
- **URL:** https://edermunizz.itch.io/pixel-art-rpg-character-creator
- **Sprite size:** 36x24 animation cells
- **Animations:** Walking, idle, jump, attack, defend, damage, death, special
- **Characters:** Unlimited (generator tool)
- **License:** Commercial use OK, attribution required
- **Price:** Free (donations accepted)
- **Notes:** Most complete animation set of any free option. Generator runs in-browser. Only generates right-facing sprites — best for side-view battle sprites, not overworld walking.

## Recommended Combinations

### Best Paid Combo (~$22-35)
- **NES Style TileSet** ($7) — authentic NES palette, includes battle backgrounds
- **Time Fantasy RPG Sprites** ($15) — 80+ characters, 16x16 base, SNES aesthetic
- Optional: Side-View Battlers expansion ($8) for combat animations

### Best Free Combo
- **Open RPG Fantasy** (CC0) — covers overworld, towns, dungeons, ship
- **javikolog 16x16 set** + **GrafxKid CC0 templates** — layered source files for building all 12 classes

### Best All-in-One
- **Lo-Bit RPG** ($20) — tiles AND 170+ characters, NES-styled. May want a dedicated overworld tileset to supplement.

### Best for Character Customization
- **Time Elements Character Core** ($20) or **Puny Characters Editor** ($10) — modular systems for creating all 12 character classes with unique looks

### Style Consistency Note
The finalbossblues packs (Time Fantasy, Lo-Bit, Open RPG Fantasy) are all from the same artist, so mixing them produces a consistent visual style.
