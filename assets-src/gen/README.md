# AI sprite generation

Character sprites are generated by an image model, locked to fixed poses, then reduced to clean pixel art.
Two backends share the same poses, pixelize step, cache and review output:

- `--backend gemini` (Google, default model `gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image`, needs `GEMINI_API_KEY` in the
  git-ignored `.env` and billing enabled on the Google project; the free tier has no quota for image models).
  Each request sends the pose-guide sheet, a crop of the style reference (`refs/style_reference.jpg`,
  git-ignored), and prose instructions with a description of every cell.
- `--backend bedrock` (Stability Control Structure on Amazon Bedrock, us-west-2, `bedrock` AWS profile).

Every paid request goes through a budget guard (see `docs/ART_2X_PLAN.md`, hard rule 1):
- `--backend` is required.
- Uncached requests need `--paid`, and at most `--max-paid` (default 1) are allowed per run.
- Each paid attempt is written to `cache/ledger.jsonl` before the call; failures count as spent.
- Any earlier attempt at the same character+sheet blocks a new paid call unless `--retry` is passed.

```bash
python3 -I assets-src/gen/selftest.py                                                    # offline checks
python3 -I assets-src/gen/generate.py knight --backend gemini --sheets battle --seeds 1234 --dry-run
python3 -I assets-src/gen/generate.py knight --backend gemini --sheets battle --seeds 1234 --paid
python3 -I assets-src/gen/generate.py --repixelize assets-src/gen/out/gemini/knight/1234   # offline
```

Outputs go to `assets-src/gen/out/<backend>/<id>/<seed>/` (git-ignored): `<sheet>_guide.png`,
`<sheet>_raw.png`, `<sheet>_prompt.txt` / `_inputN.png` (dry run), `<sheet>.png` (native pixel sheet) and
`review.png`. Responses are cached in `cache/` by request hash.

**What is committed:** per character, `approved/<id>/{battle,field}.png` (the cut native sheets) and their `.json`
(cache key, flips, `head_from` fixes), plus the matching `cache/<key>.{png,json}` (the raw Gemini render, kept so
sheets can be re-cut offline and the self-test can prove each approved request still reproduces its key) and
`cache/ledger.jsonl`. Everything else (`out/`, rejected attempts in `cache/`) is local scratch. Store a reviewed
sheet with `python3 -I assets-src/gen/approve.py <id> <battle|field> [--flip left] [--flip-frames left:0]`.
`approved/knight/field_down.png` stays because the knight's approved field request used it as an identity input.

## Pipeline

1. **Spec** (`spec.py`) holds everything shared by all characters: the style prompt, negatives, grid sizes and the
   exact joint positions of every pose (32x40 pose units, feet on y=37).
2. **Guides** (`guides.py`) draw each pose as a grey mannequin. Far limbs are light, near limbs dark, and the head
   has a face-direction cue. All poses for a sheet go on one image, so the model draws one consistent character.
3. **Generate** (`bedrock.py`) calls Stable Image Control Structure with the guide, the shared style prompt, the
   character prompt from `characters.yaml` and a fixed seed.
4. **Pixelize** (`pixelize.py`):
   - Flood-fill keys out the background, and blobs that don't overlap the guide (invented props) are dropped.
   - A palette is k-means fitted on the full-resolution figure and snapped to 15-bit colour.
   - Each output pixel takes the most common palette colour in its block, with near-black ink taking priority.
   - Speckles are majority-filtered and a 1px outline is added.

## Sheets (1 art px = 2 screen px)

| Sheet | Layout | Frames | Native cell | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `battle` | 3x2, 1536x1024 (3:2) | ready, attack, cast, hurt, down, victory (facing left) | 64x64, feet on row 61 | 128x128 |
| `field_down` | 3x1 strip, 768x512 (3:2) | stand, step L, step R, facing the viewer | 24x48, feet on row 46 | 48x96 |
| `field_up` | 3x1 | same, seen from behind | 24x48 | 48x96 |
| `field_left` | 3x1 | same, side view facing left (right = mirror) | 24x48 | 48x96 |

Gemini requests send: the pose guide, a crop of `refs/style_reference.jpg`, and (optionally) `identity_ref` from
`characters.yaml` upscaled 8x. Backgrounds are requested as solid magenta and keyed by colour; older white
renders fall back to a border flood fill.

**Monster cutter (`monster.cut`, `split_cells`).** A 3-pose monster raw is split at the figure gaps, not at fixed
thirds: a 4-connected foreground piece crossing a third line moves whole into the cell holding most of its pixels
(the cell widens, everything else in the widened band becomes key colour, and the cell's left offset keeps lunges
relative to the idle). Nothing crossing = the thirds byte for byte; a piece with more than `CROSS_SHARE` (30 %) on
the minority side (two figures fused) keeps the thirds. `cut(..., split="thirds")` is the old cutter, which every
approved sheet was cut with (approved sheets are copies, never re-cut). `frame_report` adds `flat_edges` (per frame,
`(x, y0, y1)` runs of 3+ edge pixels with no outline ink, open to the side: a slice) and `edges_ok`; `info["split"]`
lists the moved pieces. Selftest: `selftest_cut.py`.

## Adding a character

Add an entry to `characters.yaml` with `prompt` (costume only), `battle_gear`, `field_gear` and `seeds`. Generate,
review `review.png`, and move the approved seed to the front of `seeds`.

## Monster rig body templates (docs/ENEMY_ANIM_PLAN.md 4.1)

A rig file `anim/monsters/<sprite>.yaml` picks `body: biped | quadruped | bird`. The biped template is in `rig.py`; the
beast templates (T5a) are in `rig_templates.py`, whose docstring is the reference for the joint names. In short
(`_n` = near side, `_f` = far side, image coordinates, facing right):

| Body | Spine / head | Tail | Limbs | Wings (only when marked) |
|------|--------------|------|-------|--------------------------|
| `quadruped` (wolf, gr_wolf, dragon) | `hip` (root) → `back` → `chest` (`body` chain) → `neck` → `head` (`neck` chain) → `snout` (`head` rigid) | `tail0` → `tail1` → `tail2` → `tail_tip` (`tail` chain) | fore `sh` → `el` → `paw` → `ftoe`; hind `hip_n/f` → `knee` → `foot` → `toe` | `wsh` → `wel` → `wtip` from `chest` |
| `bird` (cockatrice) | `hip` (root) → `chest` (`body` torso) → `neck` → `head` (`neck` chain) → `beak` | same `tail` chain (the snake tail) | `hip_n/f` → `knee` → `foot` → `toe` | `wsh` → `wel` → `wtip` from `chest` |

- Chain parts (`body`, `neck`, `tail`) are mesh-warped with `width: auto`, so the warp covers every pixel the part
  segments to; override `chain` for more joints (e.g. a longer snake tail) and add parts such as a jaw
  (`parts: {jaw: {bone: [head, jaw]}}`) as usual.
- Pins: wind-up and recover pin every knee / elbow and foot / paw; follow, hurt and cast pin every foot (four on a
  quadruped); `atk_mid` plants both hind feet (`planted:` overrides, a string or a list). A rearing hurt (wolf, dragon)
  must release the forepaws in `frames: {hurt_in1: {pins: [foot_n, foot_f]}, hurt_in2: {pins: [foot_n, foot_f]}}`,
  otherwise the forelegs hold the chest down.
- Mark the toes: a paw part then rides its pinned ankle without rotating, so it stays on the ground line.
- A pinned foot never moves: when the hip would rise out of the leg's reach, `rig.solve` moves the body toward the
  foot instead of letting the IK lift it.
- A `cloth` part may give its `chain` per keyframe (`chain: {idle: [...], attack: [...]}`); its joints join the wind
  and cast move sets like a plain chain.
- `breath_joint:` moves the `idle_b` cut (default `hip`).
- `frames.idle_b.tweaks: {joint: [dx, dy]}` (FW-6, any body; every component at most 1 px, more raises): idle_b is
  then a rig frame at t 0 from idle with those joints moved (wing flutter, robe sway), followed by the body's usual
  idle_b op (the bob, the floating sink or the blob sag). Without tweaks idle_b is byte-identical to before.

### FW-5 template options (`rig.py`: `OPT_PARENT`, `OPT_PARTS`, `Rig._fw5`; selftest `selftest_fw5.py`)

Optional template parts are added only when a rig marks every joint of the part in at least one keyframe (or names
the part under `parts`), so rigs that mark none are byte-identical; `Rig.optional` lists the ones in use.

| Option | Joints (parent) | Parts, z | Recipe effect | Prototype |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| biped wings | `wsh_<s>` (chest) → `wel_<s>` → `wtip_<s>`, s = n, f | `uwing_<s>` (r 6), `fwing_<s>` (r 8), behind everything | the wing joints join the wind / follow / cast sets | `gargoyle`, `chaos` |
| biped arm pairs | `sh_<s>` (chest) → `el_<s>` → `hand_<s>`, s = n2 f2 n3 f3 | `uarm_<s>`, `farm_<s>`; near pairs before `uarm_n`, far pairs before `uarm_f` | `rig.arms` gains them (cast set); `attack_hand` / `half_hand` / `cast_hand` take lists | `chaos` |
| quadruped heads | `neck<i>` (chest) → `head<i>` → `snout<i>` (+ `jaw<i>` as a rig part), i = 2..5 | `neck<i>` chain `[chest, neck<i>, head<i>]`, `head<i>` rigid; before `neck` | symbolic `head` = every head; every recipe set moves them; heads outside `lead_heads` (default `[1]`) lag at half t in `atk_wind1/2` (`monster_anim.LAG_FRAMES`) | `fire_hydra` (green), `hydra` (open) |
| `preset: mechanical` | - | parts must be `rigid` / `torso` / `prop` / `fx`; sampler `nearest` in the rig and every frame (else `ValueError`) | rotation cap 15° on every part (`anim_rules.rotation_cap(rots, cap)`, frame key `rot_cap`) | `warmech` (open) |

### Roster templates (docs/ROSTER_PLAN.md 5.2; each module's docstring is the joint reference)

| Body (module) | Root / contacts (pinned every frame) | Joints and default parts | Recipe notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `blob` (`rig_blob.py`, FW-6) | `base` (ground: ignores dy and tweaks); mark it at the top of the planted band | `base → mid → top` = `body` (`kind: shear`); pods/arms `mid → sh_<s> → el_<s> → hand_<s>` (`uarm_<s>`, `farm_<s>`, s = n, f, drawn only when marked) | squash per frame (below); `idle_b` = one interior row removed (sag); `head` = `top` |
| `serpent` (`rig_serpent.py`, FW-2) | `c_f` (root), `c_n` (coil ground contacts = feet, judged per foot), `body0`, `t1` (all ground) | `coil` rigid `c_f → c_n`; `body` chain `body0 → body1 → neck`; `head` rigid `head → snout` (+ `jaw`); `tail` chain `t1 → t2 → t3 → tail_tip` | moves = `body1`, neck/head/snout/jaw, tail; `idle_b` above `neck`; `head` = head + snout. A tail raised only in the attack key: mark it only there with `parts: {tail: {in: [attack]}}` |
| `rooted` (`rig_serpent.py`, FW-2 preset) | `root` (feet), `maw`, every `k<i>_0` (ground: the mass never turns) | `mass` rigid `root → maw`; `tent<i>` chains `k<i>_0 → k<i>_1 → k<i>_2 → k<i>_3`, i = 1..6, each on its own root | rig key `lead: [i, ...]`: the lead tentacles move with the recipe's t, the others with half (sway); daze droops via the shared dy |
| `arthropod` (`rig_arthropod.py`, FW-4) | `body` (root); every `tip<i>_<s>` is a foot (pinned in every recipe, judged per foot) | `thorax` rigid `body → head`; `fangs` `head → fang`; `abdomen` rigid `abd → abd_end` (from `body`); `tail` chain `abd_end → tl0 → tl1 → tl2 → tl3 → sting`; claws `sh_/el_/hand_{n,f}` from `body`; legs `coxa<i>_<s> → knee<i>_<s> → tip<i>_<s>` = `leg<i>_<s>` chain (i = 1..4 front to back), each an IK limb | moves = body, head, fang, abdomen, tail, claws (knees follow by IK); `head` = head; `idle_b` above `body`. Mark only the legs the art shows |

**`squash` frame op (FW-6, `anim_squash.py`).** `frames.<f>.squash: {sx, sy}` (any body; 0.80-1.25 each, sx·sy
0.85-1.15), `auto`, or `null` (turns a template squash off). Whole interior rows and columns are removed or duplicated
about the lowest row and the anchor column (the solved `base` x, else the bottom-band centre); the 2 outermost rows
and columns are never touched; per band the straightest line is taken (deterministic); an interior pixel left on the
edge is re-inked with the outline colour. `auto` sizes the frame toward the S→T lerp (height, and the bottom band's
left/right extents; columns only for unpinned frames, never past the cell border). The feet check scales the base
reference by sx about the anchor and the bbox-height check scales the lerp by sy for explicit squashes; `auto` frames
are judged by the plain lerp. `kind: shear` parts slide each row sideways by the chain's solved x displacement at its
height (no resampling, no vertical motion; the lean is capped at 25°). Blob defaults: wind1/wind2 `{sx 1.04, sy 0.94}`
/ `{1.08, 0.88}` with no dy, atk_mid / atk_follow / hurt_in2 `auto`, recover `{0.97, 1.03}`, hurt_in1 `{1.10, 0.90}`,
daze / daze_b sy 0.94 / 0.92. Prototypes: `green_slime` (wind t 0.10/0.25 coil forward, follow settle 0.80),
`gray_ooze` (atk_mid t 0.10 with the fist riding, hurt_in1 `moves: [mid, top]`).

**Serpent recipe (cobra prototype).** The shared wind barely moves a hooded head: translate the upper body instead
(`atk_wind1: {dy: 0, tweaks: {body1: [-1, 0], neck: [-1, 0], head: [-2, 1], snout: [-2, 1]}}`, wind2 about twice
that with `t: -0.25`); bending only the neck tears it. When the attack arch turns more than the 25° chain cap at 0.35,
use `atk_mid: {t: 0.20, moves: [neck, head, snout]}` and lower the head by tweak if it rises out of the lerp height.

**Arthropod recipe (tarantula, scorpion, black_widow prototypes).** Never interpolate legs (their keys are 40-60° apart):
`moves: [body, head, fang, abd, abd_end]` (+ tail and claw joints for a scorpion) in mid / follow / recover / hurt, so
the body moves, the tips hold and the IK bends the knees. Mask the abdomen per keyframe (legs drawn over it ride it).
A leg whose tip steps between the keys (tarantula leg 2): add its knee and tip to `atk_mid.moves` and leave it out of
`pins`. Pins only on tips grounded in the attack key when the attack lifts the body (`atk_mid.pins: [tip2_f]`).
Small pockets that close between bent legs (4-7 px `new hole`) are inked shut with `paint`; a pincer gap is not
(keep the claw out of `moves` instead). daze: `moves: [head]`, `dy: 0 / 1` so a raised tail keeps its height. Open
issue: a rear-up hurt key (scorpion, black_widow: idle-hurt IoU 0.28-0.29) can't bridge `hurt_in1 -> hurt_in2`
continuity; see the FW-4 hand-off.

## Monster animation (docs/ENEMY_ANIM_PLAN.md, docs/ENEMY_ANIM_ISSUES.md)

Adding a new monster end to end (Gemini sheet, rig, checks, image review, play test, troubleshooting): `docs/NEW_MONSTER_GUIDE.md`.

In-betweens are cut from the approved monster pixels by `monster_anim.py` (no AI, no Bedrock, no paid requests); the
approved sheets are never modified. One rig per base sprite in `anim/monsters/<sprite>.yaml`; palette variants reuse it.

```bash
python3 -I assets-src/gen/monster_anim.py mark <id>            # zoomed keyframes with joints and part labels
python3 -I assets-src/gen/monster_anim.py check --all          # every check + lock files: only "OK" lines
python3 -I assets-src/gen/monster_anim.py review <id> ...      # gen/out/anim/review/monsters/ (strips, onion, GIFs, _stage)
python3 -I assets-src/gen/monster_anim.py lock <id>            # <id>.lock.json, only after the image review passes
python3 -I assets-src/build_assets.py monsters_ai              # the build runs the same checks (efix gates on)
```

- Rig fixes go in the rig: masks, `z`, `frames.<f>` (`tweaks`, `pins`, `dy`, `drop`, `moves`, `scale`, `sampler`),
  `holds` (at most 1 per sequence) and `paint` (`{frame, xy: [[x, y], ...], rgb: [r, g, b] | null}`, native frame
  pixels; use a colour from the surrounding approved palette). Review-strip coordinates are 4x: native = (px − 2) / 4.
- `frames.<f>.scale: {part: s}` (0.25 ≤ s ≤ 1) is for `kind: fx` parts only; `frames.<f>.sampler: nearest |
  rotsprite` overrides the rig's `sampler` in one frame (`monster_render.py`).
- Checks: `anim_checks.py` (5.1 checks 1-14), `anim_efix.py` and `monster_check.py` (F1 fx exclusion, F3 fx-mask
  integrity, F4 intra-part crease, F5 foot release, F7 prop attached; F6 edge roughness is report only). The fx parts
  are an allowlist in `selftest_efix.py`: garland `glow`, lich `trail` and `crystal`, vampire `slash`, hollow_seraph
  `halo`. `frames.<f>.brighten: {fxpart: n}` (FW-7, n 1..3, fx only) steps the part n colours up its own approved
  ramp (warm and cool hues apart, by luminance; palette-safe); the seraph halo brightens in `cast_gather` / `cast_release`.
- A frame whose bytes change fails `check` until it is reviewed and re-locked. The monster atlas must be identical
  under any `PYTHONHASHSEED`, and a `monsters_ai` build must leave the party atlases byte-identical.
- Frame tables and recipes live in `monster_recipes.py` (pass F). Every rig gets `daze` / `daze_b` (sleep, stun) from
  one shared recipe whose symbolic `head` tweak resolves per body template (`rig.BODY[...]["head"]`; a missing joint is
  an error); a rig may override `frames.daze`, or hold `daze` (it then shows `hurt_in1`). A boss with an approved
  `boss_attack_raise` gets `attack_raise` plus `atk_raise_in`, `atk_raise_b` (a 1 px bob of the raise), `atk_swing`,
  `atk_swing2` and the `heavy` continuity sequence; the atlas writes `hasRaise`, `opaqueRight` and `head` per sheet.
- `python3 -I assets-src/gen/selftest.py` covers the monster pipeline (`selftest_anim`, `selftest_beasts`,
  `selftest_fixes`, `selftest_efix`, including the real-lich crystal size check: `cast_rise` ≤ 40 % of `cast`).
